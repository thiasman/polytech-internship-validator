# Polytech Orléans — 5th Year (5A) Electronics & Optics Engineering Syllabus & Internship (PFE) Rubric

## 1. Institutional Overview
Polytech Orléans (University of Orléans, Réseau Polytech) awards the French *Diplôme d'Ingénieur* (Master of Science in Engineering, Grade de Master). Every 5th-year (5A) engineering student must complete a mandatory end-of-studies engineering internship (*Projet de Fin d'Études — PFE*) before graduation.

## 2. Mandatory Administrative & Legal Requirements
- **Minimum Duration**: 20 to 26 weeks full-time (5 to 6 months).
- **Minimum Legal Stipend (Gratification)**: Must meet or exceed the French statutory minimum (~650 EUR/month for full-time internships).
- **Company Registration**: Valid French SIRET number (14 digits) or verified international corporate registration, active commercial status, and physical engineering facility.
- **Industrial Supervision**: Must be supervised on-site by a qualified engineer or R&D scientist (*Tuteur industriel*).
- **Confidentiality & Defense Clearance**: If the internship involves Defense, Aerospace, or dual-use technologies under a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA / *Stage Confidentiel*), the academic jury must be notified in advance so the student's thesis defense (*Soutenance*) is scheduled under closed-door (*Huis clos*) protocol.

## 3. Pedagogical & Technical Requirements (5A Electronics & Optics Track)
A 5th-year engineering internship MUST require autonomous Master-level engineering design, modeling, simulation, architecture, or R&D validation.

### 3.1 Qualifying Master-Level Engineering Competencies (GREEN — Ready to Approve)
An internship qualifies for 5A Electronics & Optics validation if the core mission includes at least two of the following:
- **Optical & Photonic System Design**: Optical modeling, ray-tracing, beam-shaping, or tolerance analysis using tools such as Zemax OpticStudio, Code V, COMSOL, or MATLAB/Python.
- **Embedded Systems & Digital Architecture**: FPGA/SoC design (VHDL, Verilog, SystemVerilog, Xilinx Vivado), microcontroller firmware architecture (C/C++, RTOS), or high-speed digital signal processing (DSP).
- **Analog / Mixed-Signal / RF Circuit Design**: Schematic architecture, SPICE simulation, PCB layout design with signal integrity constraints (Altium Designer, Cadence), and quantitative lab characterization.
- **Experimental R&D & System Integration**: Designing test benches, automating optical/electronic characterization protocols, statistical signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) analysis, and prototyping hardware-software systems.

### 3.2 Non-Qualifying Technician / Execution Tasks (YELLOW — Flag for Academic Review)
An internship MUST BE FLAGGED (`YELLOW`) if the mission consists primarily of technician-level execution, production-line operation, or quality-control tasks without autonomous engineering design, such as:
- Manual PCB assembly, through-hole or SMD soldering, rework, or cable harnessing.
- Following pre-written factory test procedures (e.g., standard multimeter/oscilloscope pass/fail checks on a production line) without designing the test architecture or circuit.
- Routine inventory logging, component stocking, Excel data entry, or basic technical support.
- Any company with a prior history of assigning technician-level tasks to 5A students in the Polytech Orléans historical internship database.
